Does the readiness check cost anything?

No. It's free, takes about 2 minutes and doesn't check your credit. Monica replies to you personally.

No. The check never touches your credit report or score. It asks you for an approximate score only to estimate your options.

Your answers go to one local real estate agent, Monica Polo. Your information is never sold.

What does the check ask about?

Money and timing. It never asks about who you are. Monica follows Fair Housing rules.

Which areas does Monica serve?

San Antonio and Bexar County, plus Converse, Schertz, Cibolo, Universal City, Live Oak, Selma, New Braunfels, Boerne and Helotes.

How do I find out what my home is worth?

Take the free seller check, or enter your address in the home value section on the home page. Monica sends you a personal estimate within one business day.

Can I sell and buy at the same time?

Yes. The check lets you answer for both, so you can see how the sale could fund your next home.

What loan options should I ask about when buying?

Depending on your situation, compare conventional, FHA and VA loans, and ask a lender whether any down payment assistance programs fit you. If you or your spouse serve at Joint Base San Antonio, ask Monica about buying with VA benefits.

What are the steps to buy a home in San Antonio?

Most purchases follow the same path: get your finances in order, talk with a lender about loan options, search for homes, make an offer, complete the inspection and option period, and close with a title company. Monica explains each step before you get to it.

What are the steps to sell a home in San Antonio?

Selling typically means pricing the home with local sales in mind, preparing it for photos and showings, reviewing offers, handling the buyer's inspection and option period, and closing with a title company. Monica walks you through each step.
