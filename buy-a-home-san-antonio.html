The free readiness check asks about your savings, income, debts and timing. It takes about 2 minutes and never touches your credit. You get a readiness score, a realistic price range and a clear next step, and Monica replies to you personally.

What buying in San Antonio usually looks like

Most purchases follow the same path: get your finances in order, talk with a lender about loan options, search for homes in the neighborhoods you like, make an offer, complete the inspection and option period, and close with a title company. Monica explains each step before you get to it, so there are no surprises.

Loan options to ask about

Depending on your situation, you may want to compare conventional, FHA and VA loans, and ask a lender whether any down payment assistance programs fit you. If you or your spouse serve at Joint Base San Antonio, ask Monica about buying with VA benefits.
