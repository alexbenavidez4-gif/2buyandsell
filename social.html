Why no "log in here" box?

Typing your Instagram or Facebook password into another website isn't safe, and it breaks those apps' rules. It's also how accounts get locked or hacked. Here, each button opens the real app or site. Your phone or browser keeps you logged in, so it's still one tap, and your accounts stay safe.

Want to post to all of them at once? That takes a scheduling service (like Buffer or Postiz) that each app has approved. We can add that later.
